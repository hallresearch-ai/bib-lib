## Scope and method

This is a deliberately narrow audit. It does not assess normalization, capitalization, general metadata completeness, link availability, or bibliographic accuracy.

- A missing URL means the `url` field is absent or blank. A DOI or link in another field does not make the strict URL check pass.
- Duplicate candidates were detected by comparing titles after removing braces, punctuation, whitespace, and letter case.
- Shared DOI and URL values were checked separately because they can expose duplicates or incorrect metadata.
- The initial duplicate and missing-field counts were computed locally. The URL-research follow-up below used external scholarly indexes and authoritative web sources.
